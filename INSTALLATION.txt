Aegis AI — Overview Hero Compact Size / Wolf & Orb Focus Fix
==============================================================

This is a CSS-only patch built against the previously delivered
Aegis_AI_Page_Hero_Backgrounds_Implemented_Changed_Files.zip.

Install:
1. Back up your current TankWeb-v3/public/assessment/aegis-page-heroes.css.
2. Extract this zip. Copy the included public/assessment/aegis-page-heroes.css
   into TankWeb-v3/public/assessment/aegis-page-heroes.css (overwrite).
3. Hard-refresh your browser with Ctrl+Shift+R.
4. Open Command Center > Overview and verify the compact layout, animated
   wolf and orbiting rings on both desktop and mobile. Check other pages.

What changed:
- Restore position:absolute to overview glow orbs. The prior selector
  accidentally made them position:relative and stretched the hero.
- Reduce the large background shield intensity so the existing wolf dominates.
- Enhance wolf glow and contrast of the original orbit rings.
- Correct the mobile wolf caption spacing.

No changes to HTML, JavaScript, model, backend, database, or other heroes.
The original floating wolf, moving glow orbs, and rotating orbit rings remain.

Local isolated visual checks: 1878px, 1440px, 390px, 320px. Before: 673px
hero on desktop; after: 431px. Wolf/logo/ring animations confirmed in
computed CSS; no horizontal overflow in isolated preview. Hosted integration
still needs verification after installation.
