Interpretation guidance
Performance ≠ field accuracy

A balanced, simulated evaluation dataset does not establish performance against unseen operational attacks.

Traceable but not causal

Threshold indicators describe observed feature values. They are not SHAP explanations or proof of a vulnerability.

Advisory by design

Risk scores and anomaly flags are intended to support human investigation, not trigger automatic sanctions.
